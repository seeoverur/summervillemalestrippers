About This Guide

A short, independent directory of adult nightlife around Summerville, SC, written for groups planning bachelorette parties, birthdays, and girls' nights.

Summerville doesn't have strip clubs of its own, and male revue nights around Charleston come and go, so groups usually end up piecing a night together from touring shows, North Charleston clubs, local bars, and private dancers. This guide keeps all of that on one page and sticks to places and performers that are actually around.

Local performers we work with include Michael At Your Place, a Charleston-based entertainer who's a regular at Summerville bachelorette parties.

If you'd rather skip the planning, we can also line up a private show at your house, rental, or hotel. The details are on the private party page. Everyone attending must be 18 or older.
